2026

  • Every Ablation Is a Dose: Counterweights and the Semblance of Self-Repair

    Areeb Ahmad, Pratinav Seth, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu. Preprint, arXiv:2610.02173

    Reads every ablation as a point on a dose axis λ and shows that a downstream unit's repair response follows an affine law whose slope is a gain present before any ablation and predictable from the weights.

  • Beyond Components: Singular Vector-Based Interpretability of Transformer Circuits

    Areeb Ahmad*, Abhinav Joshi*, Ashutosh Modi. NeurIPS 2025

    Decomposes attention heads and MLPs into orthogonal singular directions. On IOI, gendered-pronoun and greater-than circuits, single functional heads turn out to carry several overlapping subfunctions.

  • Calibration Across Layers: Understanding Calibration Evolution in LLMs

    Abhinav Joshi, Areeb Ahmad, Ashutosh Modi. EMNLP 2025

    Tracks confidence layer by layer: models become overconfident, then recalibrate in the upper layers after the decision is settled. A low-dimensional residual-stream direction improves calibration without hurting accuracy.

  • Towards Quantifying Commonsense Reasoning with Mechanistic Insights

    Abhinav Joshi, Areeb Ahmad, Divyaksh Shukla, Ashutosh Modi. NAACL 2025

    Builds activity graphs that generate very large question sets for commonsense reasoning, then uses path patching to locate where in the model the decision is made and reinforced.
